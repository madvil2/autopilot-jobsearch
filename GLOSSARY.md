# Glossary

The words the skills, docs and templates use. One meaning each.

**Workspace**: a clone of this repository with the personal files created by `/init`. The folder Claude Code runs in.

**Profile**: `profile.md`. The facts about the person and their personal rules. The only source of facts for forms, messages, CVs and prep.

**Rules**: `rules.md`. How the agent behaves on anyone's behalf: writing, applying, replying, scheduling, tracking. Impersonal; personal rules live in the Profile.

**Source**: one place the Sweep looks, listed in `sources.md`: an inbox to answer, the tracker's own job index, or a board of postings.

**Posting**: one job advertisement on a Source, identified by its URL or the platform's vacancy id.

**Sweep**: one full pass over mail, messages, follow-ups, the tracker index and every Source, with applications, replies, bookings and a Report. The unit of the twice-daily routine.

**Application**: one company's process in the tracker (JobCubby), from saved through applied, screening, interview and offer, or rejected. One per company unless the Rules say otherwise.

**Event**: a dated note on an Application's timeline: a reply sent, a call booked, a test received, a salary named.

**Interview**: a scheduled call or meeting attached to an Application, with a round (screening, technical, final), a time, a link and an interviewer. Exists in the tracker and in the calendar.

**Follow-up**: one short nudge sent on an Application whose follow-up date passed with no answer, only where a human thread exists.

**Rejection reason**: one of five fixed strings recorded when an Application is rejected: Rejected at CV screening, Position closed or other candidates, Rejected after screening call, Rejected after interview, Withdrew (my decision).

**CV version**: one markdown file in `cv/`, rendered to one PDF. `base.md` is the master; other files are variants per market, language or role.

**Cover letter**: one markdown file in `cover-letters/` for one Posting, in the three-paragraph structure, rendered to PDF.

**Prep**: `prep/<company>.md`, the file written before an Interview: company facts, intro, why-company, likely questions, my questions, checklist, and the debrief after.

**Debrief**: the analysis of an Interview recording against the coach method, plus the facts it revealed. Written into the Prep and the Application.

**Report**: the fixed-format summary a Sweep saves to `reports/`: needs your attention, replied, rejections, applied, skipped.

**Stack radar**: the count of technologies across the requirements of recent Applications, compared with the CV version, with proposed tags and a learning plan.

**Market**: a country or platform group with its own conventions for the CV, location, phone and salary figure (for example DE, remote EU, a regional board).
